# Style grounding lists

Pick ONE entry per slot and vary picks between projects; the point is to push
the model into a specific, physical latent space instead of its polished
default.

## Photographic

**Lens** (one):
- "Shot on ARRI Signature Prime 47mm" — natural, documentary
- "Shot on Cooke S4/i 75mm" — organic skin, warm, cinematic
- "Shot on Canon K-35 T1.4" — vintage 70s warmth, glowing highlights

**Film stock / texture** (one):
- "Kodak Portra 400"
- "Kodak Vision3 500T"
- "Cinestill 800T"
- "authentic film grain, subtle halation, slight JPEG artifacting, unedited snapshot"

**Lighting** (one):
- "Chiaroscuro / Rembrandt lighting"
- "Gobo shadow projection from window blinds"
- "Motivated volumetric lighting / god rays"
- "Negative fill deepening the shadows"
- "Harsh direct flash"

For projection, prefer lighting that makes the *subject* the light source
(motivated, volumetric, halation) — it reads as emitted light on the wall.

## Illustrated: the 8-layer style stack

Build the style as Format > Medium > Linework > Shading > Palette > Texture >
Lighting > Composition, drawing on specific movements and tactile media.

**Art movements** (examples):
- "The Ashcan School" — gritty, dark urban realism
- "Synchromism" — layered color planes
- "Precisionism" — sharp geometric realism
- "Ukiyo-e woodblock" — flat colors, bold lines, patterned backgrounds

**Tactile media** (examples):
- "Heavy charcoal sketch with smudged shadows"
- "Matte opaque gouache with visible brushstrokes"
- "3-color Risograph print with halftone dots and screenprint noise"
- "Cut paper diorama with felt textures and visible paper edges"

Flat, bold, high-contrast media (woodblock, risograph, gouache) project
especially well: crisp shapes, strong luminance against the dark surface.
